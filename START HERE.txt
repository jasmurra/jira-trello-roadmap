Jira → Trello Roadmap

This runs on your Mac in a browser tab. Sign in with your own Jira and Trello
accounts. There are no shared passwords in this folder.

Install
1. Unzip this folder anywhere (Downloads is fine).
2. Double-click start.command.
3. If macOS blocks it: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
4. Click Sign In and complete Jira and Trello (Duo is fine).
   This Mac keeps that session so you usually do not sign in again.
5. Pick your Jira board (and filter) and your Trello board, then Compare.

You need Python 3 on the Mac (the python3 command). First launch creates a
local .venv and may download Chromium for the sign-in window.

Updates
After this first zip, you do not need another zip.
• Restart start.command, or
• Click Check for updates on the page.

Your login, hidden issues, and board choices stay on this computer.
